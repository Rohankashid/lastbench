# BXE 2024 Pattern Oct/Nov 2025 Solved Question Paper Architecture

## 1. Exam Metadata
- **Subject**: Basic Electronics Engineering
- **Course Code**: `ESC-101-ETC` (2024 Pattern, Semester I)
- **Paper Code**: `PC-5205`, `[6351]-114`
- **Session**: Oct / Nov 2025 (Examination held Jan 2025 / Winter Session)
- **Max Marks**: 70 Marks (Time: 2.5 Hours)
- **Structure**: 10 Questions arranged as 5 Choice Pairs (Q1 OR Q2, Q3 OR Q4, Q5 OR Q6, Q7 OR Q8, Q9 OR Q10)
- **Target Category**: `pyq`

## 2. All 10 Questions (30 Sub-questions) Content Mapping
1. **Q1**:
   - `a` [5M]: Impact of Electronics on Industry and Society (Automation, IoT, Healthcare, Communication, Transport, Agriculture).
   - `b` [5M]: Bridge Rectifier circuit diagram, operation, waveforms ($V_{dc}, V_{rms}, \eta, \gamma$).
   - `c` [4M]: Compare Active and Passive Components (Energy gain, external bias, control, examples).
2. **Q2**:
   - `a` [5M]: Half-Wave Rectifier circuit diagram, operation, waveforms.
   - `b` [5M]: V-I Characteristics of P-N Junction Diode (Forward bias, knee voltage, reverse saturation, breakdown).
   - `c` [4M]: Compare LED and Photodiode (Light emission vs detection, biasing, optical mechanism, applications).
3. **Q3**:
   - `a` [5M]: N-Well Method of VLSI CMOS Manufacturing (Twin-tub / N-well sequence, twin active areas, LOCOS/STI, implantation).
   - `b` [5M]: N-Channel Enhancement MOSFET Construction & Working (Threshold voltage $V_{th}$, inversion layer channel formation, pinch-off).
   - `c` [4M]: Numerical: $I_B = 100\;\mu\text{A}$, $I_C = 5.65\text{ mA} \implies \beta = 56.5$, $I_E = 5.75\text{ mA}$.
4. **Q4**:
   - `a` [5M]: MOSFET as a Switch (Cutoff $V_{GS} < V_{th} \implies R_{OFF} \approx \infty, V_o = V_{DD}$; Saturation / Triode $V_{GS} > V_{th} \implies R_{ON} \approx 0, V_o \approx 0\text{ V}$).
   - `b` [5M]: Output Characteristics of BJT in CE Configuration (Active, Saturation, Cutoff regions, Early effect).
   - `c` [4M]: Numerical: $I_B = 50\;\mu\text{A}$, $I_C = 3.65\text{ mA} \implies \beta = 73$, $I_E = 3.70\text{ mA}$.
5. **Q5**:
   - `a` [5M]: Classification of Logic Gates (Basic, Universal, Special) + Detailed Operation of AND & OR gates with symbols, truth tables, and Boolean equations.
   - `b` [5M]: Functional Block Diagram of Microprocessor (ALU, Register Array, Control Unit, System Buses).
   - `c` [4M]: Number Conversions: (i) $(110011)_2 = (51)_{10}$, (ii) $(75.371)_{10} \approx (1001011.010111)_2$ (step-by-step successive multiplication).
6. **Q6**:
   - `a` [5M]: State & Prove De Morgan's Theorems ($\overline{A+B} = \bar{A}\bar{B}$ and $\overline{A\cdot B} = \bar{A}+\bar{B}$) via Truth Tables and Logic Equivalences.
   - `b` [5M]: Full Adder Circuit Design (Truth table, Boolean expressions $S = A \oplus B \oplus C_{in}$, $C_{out} = AB + BC_{in} + AC_{in}$, Logic diagram using XOR, AND, OR).
   - `c` [4M]: Compare Microprocessor and Microcontroller (CPU vs SoC, internal vs external RAM/ROM, cost, power, architecture).
7. **Q7**:
   - `a` [5M]: Block Diagram of Digital Multimeter (DMM) & Working (Constant current source for $\Omega$, I-V converter for current, rectifier/attenuator for AC, ADC, 7-segment display).
   - `b` [5M]: Functional Block Diagram of Operational Amplifier (Dual-input balanced output differential amp, intermediate gain stage, level shifter / emitter follower, push-pull output stage).
   - `c` [4M]: Ideal vs Practical Characteristics of Op-Amp IC 741 ($A_{OL}, R_{in}, R_{out}, BW, CMRR, \text{Slew Rate}, V_{os}$).
8. **Q8**:
   - `a` [5M]: Functional Block Diagram of Function Generator (Frequency control, dual constant current sources, integrator capacitor, voltage comparator multivibrator, diode shaping network, output amplifiers).
   - `b` [5M]: Inverting Amplifier Calculation ($R_1 = 1\text{ k}\Omega, R_f = 10\text{ k}\Omega, V_{CC} = \pm 12\text{ V}, A_v = -10$):
     - (i) $V_{in} = 20\text{ mV} \implies V_o = -200\text{ mV}$ (Linear operation).
     - (ii) $V_{in} = 2.2\text{ V} \implies \text{Theoretical } V_o = -22\text{ V}$, but supply is $\pm 12\text{ V}$, so Op-Amp saturates at $-V_{sat} \approx -10.5\text{ V}$ to $-11.0\text{ V}$ (Clamping / Saturation justification).
   - `c` [4M]: Block Diagram of Regulated DC Power Supply (Step-down transformer, rectifier, filter, voltage regulator, load).
9. **Q9**:
   - `a` [5M]: Sensor Selection Criteria (Range, Sensitivity, Accuracy, Resolution, Linearity, Environmental ruggedness, Cost, Interfacing).
   - `b` [5M]: Electromagnetic (EM) Frequency Spectrum & Applications (ELF, VLF, LF, MF, HF, VHF, UHF, SHF, EHF, Infrared, Visible light).
   - `c` [4M]: Active vs Passive Sensors (External excitation vs self-generating, examples: Thermocouple/Piezoelectric vs RTD/LVDT/Strain Gauge).
10. **Q10**:
    - `a` [5M]: IoT-based Data Acquisition Architecture (Sensor node array, microcontroller/ADC, communication gateway, internet cloud, analytics dashboard).
    - `b` [5M]: GSM System Architecture (Mobile Station ME+SIM, Base Station Subsystem BTS+BSC, Network Switching Subsystem MSC+HLR+VLR+EIR, PSTN interface).
    - `c` [4M]: Basic Communication System Block Diagram (Information source, input transducer, transmitter, physical channel + noise source, receiver, output transducer).
